Meet the team.

A passionate group of people serving WCC and the people of Wembley with everything they've got.

Senior Leadership

Our Lead Pastors

Senior Pastor
Ps. Soke Mun Ho

Soke Mun co-founded WCC in 1992. A dentist by profession, she answered God's call to plant a church in Wembley and has faithfully led the congregation for over 30 years. Her passion is to see every person walk in their God-given destiny.

Founding Pastor
Ps. Vanessa Richardson

Vanessa co-founded WCC in 1992 alongside Soke Mun. A gifted communicator and itinerant minister, her heart is for evangelism, discipleship and raising up strong families in the faith.
