About WCC

A community
built on love.

Wembley Christian Centre is a vibrant, multicultural Elim Pentecostal church. Since 1992, we've been serving Wembley with the love of Jesus — through worship, community and practical care.

Our vision is "Wembley and the world for Christ." Based on Isaiah 61:1-3, we Reach Out to those who don't yet know God's love, Restore the broken-hearted, and Release every person into their God-given destiny.
