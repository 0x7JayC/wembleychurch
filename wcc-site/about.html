✦ About

A family rooted
in faith.

For over two decades, Wembley Christian Centre has been a home for those seeking God, healing and genuine community.

Our Mission

Reach Out.
Restore. Release.

These three words shape everything we do. We Reach Out to those who don't yet know God's love. We Restore the broken-hearted through the power of the Holy Spirit. And we Release people into their God-given destiny.

We are Pentecostal in faith, multicultural in character, and unwavering in our pursuit of seeing every life transformed by Christ.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

✝️
Christ at the Centre

Jesus is Lord. Every gathering, every decision, every dream — centred on Him and His Word.

🕊
Spirit-Filled Life

We believe in the active, present work of the Holy Spirit in every believer's daily life.

❤️
Genuine Community

Faith is meant to be lived together. We do life with one another — in joy and in struggle.

🌍
Multicultural Family

Wembley is one of the world's most diverse communities and our church reflects that beautifully.

🙏
Powerful Prayer

Prayer is the foundation. We gather to pray together, and we live as a praying people.

🌱
Lifelong Growth

We are all on a journey — growing deeper in faith, character and our calling.

What to Expect

We can't wait to welcome you. Here's what your first Sunday at WCC looks like.

01
Arrive & Welcome

Pull up at 10:45 AM. Our welcome team will greet you and show you around.

02
Coffee & Connect

Grab a coffee in the foyer. Meet a few faces before the service starts.

03
Worship Together

Service begins at 11:00 AM — worship, prayer, the Word and presence of God.

04
Stay for Lunch

Many stay around after for a chat and the occasional shared meal.
