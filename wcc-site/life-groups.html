✦ Life Groups

Don't do life alone.

Small communities of faith meeting weekly across Wembley — for friendship, prayer, study and growth.

Why Life Groups

Where life-on-life happens.

Sundays are about gathering; Life Groups are about belonging. The best of WCC happens around dinner tables, in living rooms, in real life.

01
Real Connection

Build deep, lasting friendships with people who walk with you through every season.

02
Spiritual Growth

Dig into the Bible together, pray for one another, and grow in your faith.

03
Practical Support

When life happens — joy or struggle — your Life Group is there with you.

How It Works
Simple, warm, and life-giving.
1
Find a Group

Browse groups by area, focus or schedule below.

2
Get in Touch

Message the host — they'll welcome you in.

3
Show Up

Just come. Bring yourself, an open heart, maybe a snack.

4
Keep Coming

Most groups meet weekly. You'll quickly find your people.
